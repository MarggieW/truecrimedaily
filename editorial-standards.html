Editorial Standards

The rules behind TrueCrimeDaily.co reporting and case research.

Facts vs. allegations

Convictions, guilty pleas and documented records are stated as outcomes. Pending criminal allegations are attributed and defendants are treated as presumed innocent unless convicted.

Read →

Sources

Primary sources—court opinions, charging records, law-enforcement releases and official records—are preferred where accessible. Secondary reporting adds context, not invented certainty.

Read →

Victim-centered coverage

Graphic or sensitive facts are included only when they materially explain the evidence, investigation or court record—not for shock value.

Read →

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected transparently. Readers can contact the publication with documentation supporting a correction.
