Case File / Utah / Solved

Mackenzie Lueck: The 3 A.M. Meeting

At approximately 3 a.m. on June 17, 2019, Mackenzie Lueck met another person at Hatch Park. Police said her communications stopped at about the same moment. Investigators later placed her phone and Ayoola Ajayi’s phone at the park within less than a minute of one another.

Who was Mackenzie?

Lueck was 23 and a University of Utah student. She returned to Salt Lake City by plane around 2 a.m. and used a ride service to Hatch Park in North Salt Lake. Police said she left the park with another individual at about 3 a.m.

The digital trail

Police examined phone records, social media and communications. They said Lueck’s last communications were with Ajayi. Ajayi acknowledged earlier text conversations but initially denied meeting her or knowing what she looked like; police said his phone contained photos of her. The location records put both phones at Hatch Park, and Lueck’s phone stopped receiving data/location services.

The backyard search

A June 26 search of Ajayi’s property became a decisive turn. Neighbors told detectives they had seen him burning something with gasoline on June 17 and 18. Investigators found a fresh dig/burn area, charred items consistent with Lueck’s belongings and female human tissue. Utah State Lab testing produced a DNA profile consistent with a profile obtained from Lueck’s personal items.

Recovery and cause of death

Her remains were later recovered from a shallow grave in Logan Canyon. Prosecutors said her arms had been bound and she died from blunt-force trauma to the head. Phone data also placed Ajayi in the canyon area after the killing.

Admission and outcome

Ajayi ultimately pleaded guilty to aggravated murder and abuse/desecration of a human body. In the plea proceedings, he admitted deciding to kill Lueck before they met. He received life in prison without parole.

Why this case matters

The case demonstrates how rideshare records, handset location data, communications, witness observations, forensic excavation and DNA can converge. No single clue tells the whole story; the power came from the timeline formed by multiple independent evidence streams.
