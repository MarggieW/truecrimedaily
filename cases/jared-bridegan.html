Jared Bridegan: The Tire in the Road

On Feb. 16, 2022, Jared Bridegan stopped to move a tire deliberately placed in a dark Jacksonville Beach road. A gunman emerged and fired four shots. Two struck Bridegan; two entered the vehicle where his two-year-old daughter was sitting.

The alleged trap

Florida prosecutors say the tire was positioned to force Bridegan to stop on Sanctuary Boulevard after he had returned the twins he shared with former wife Shanna Gardner. The killing was treated as a planned ambush rather than a random roadside attack.

Henry Tenon

Authorities identified Henry Tenon as the shooter. Prosecutors said Tenon owned the blue Ford F-150 used in the crime, his DNA was found on the tire, and he had been a tenant of Mario Fernandez Saldana and Gardner. Tenon and Bridegan were strangers.

The money and communications

The State Attorney’s Office said Fernandez Saldana paid Tenon at least $10,000 after the murder and that investigators documented extensive communications between the men. Prosecutors presented these links as part of the murder-for-hire theory.

Fernandez Saldana verdict and sentence

A Duval County jury convicted Fernandez Saldana on Aug. 26, 2026 of first-degree murder and solicitation to commit a capital felony. On Nov. 12, 2026, he was sentenced to life in prison plus 30 years, with the 30-year term running concurrently.
